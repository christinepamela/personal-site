Theta

Building Breakthrough innovation inside large companies, without disrupting the Core business.

Core innovation and Breakthrough innovation are not the same game. Different rules. Different players. Different timeline. Different KPIs.

Most companies don't fail from a lack of innovation. They fail because they apply the Core playbook to Breakthrough work, and the Breakthrough dies.

Core, Edge, Beyond

Every company needs all three. Most only carry the Core.

Core

What already works. Most of the revenue, most of the attention, and on its own, not enough.

Edge

The next S-curve. Where a real breakthrough starts, and where most large companies lose it.

Beyond

The speculative bet, years out, that either becomes the next Core or teaches you something you needed to know. If it sounds like science fiction now, good. That's the Beyond zone.

Edge and Beyond are breakthrough. Core is what pays for the chance to build them.

Four things decide whether it works

Players

Mavericks aren't hired through a normal process. They're already inside most companies, usually unrecognised. Finding them is the first move.

Rules

Breakthrough moves through three stages, Chaos, Growth, Scale, and each needs a different kind of leadership and a different kind of patience.

Feedback

The signal is almost never where a company is looking. It's sitting in the part of the business built to make it go away fastest.

Incentives

What gets measured decides what survives: learning velocity, intelligent risk-taking, and real adoption, not quarterly return.
